# Google Play publishing notes

Checked 2026-09-01 against official Google Play Help pages.

- A Play Console developer account requires a one-time US$25 registration fee. The official setup page lists sign-up, agreement acceptance, fee payment, account type selection, and identity verification.
- Google Play app setup requires app name, app/game type, free/paid selection, a contact email, policy/export declarations, and acceptance of Play App Signing terms.
- Google Play uses Android App Bundles for Play distribution; Google Play generates optimized APKs from the uploaded bundle.
- Personal developer accounts created after 2023-11-13 must run a closed test with at least 12 opted-in testers continuously for at least 14 days before applying for production access.
- Direct Android installation does not require Play Store publication. The user can sideload a generated APK after enabling installation from the chosen file source, subject to Android device security prompts.
